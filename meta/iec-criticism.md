# Kritische Analyse — Verbände, Publikationen, Muster (journalistisch, 2026-11-20)

Stand: 2026-11-20 — Analysen der aktuellen Kritik an Verbänden/Standardisation-Bodies (ISO, IEC, ANSI, ITU, …) und typische Muster.

## Kritik an IEC (und ISO/ANSI)

- **„Too Bureaucratic and Slow“:** „The process of developing ISO standards being 'too bureaucratic and slow' and not responding to market needs“ — [Cambridge — Evolution of Transnational Rule-Makers through Crises]. „The process of developing standards within ISO was criticized around 2007 as being too difficult for timely completion of large and complex standards“ — [Wikipedia — ISO].
- **Komplexität:** „Standards development at this scale is: Complex, Slow, Deeply procedural“ — [SphericalCow Consulting 2026]. „Above all, patience is required.“
- **85%-Dominanz:** „Taking the ISO and IEC – which collectively account for 85 per cent of all international product standards“ — [Oxford — Demystifying private sector standards bodies]. Large private SDOs.
- **Free Access?** „Why aren't ISO standards free?“ — [Reddit r/engineering] — „International organisation with ties to UN and major industries across the globe. Why aren't these open to the public and funded by a small tax?“
- **Strategische Konkurrenz:** „Standards Development Organisations in an era of strategic competition — This risk is particularly pertinent for the two largest SDOs with global reach, the ISO and IEC“ — [USS Strategic Competition]. „Standards as geopolitical tools“ — [Outcome.co]. „A history of unethical behavior and conflict of interests“ — [Outcome.co — ISO History].
- **IEC-Spezifisch (2024–2026):** IEC Directives — IEC hat „project times reduced to 36+12 months“ — IEC war langsamer (60 Monate), ISO „SDT 36 + 9 → 36 + 12“ — „IEC now as fast as ISO“ — [IEC Directives Part 1 + IEC Supplement 2024-05]. IEC vs ANSI: „ANSI is the prevailing standards body in North America and select other regions, while IEC predominates in much of the rest of the world“ — [Construction Exec — IEC Versus ANSI].

## Typische Muster (IEC-Daten → Muster)

1. **„Publicly Available“ = Dokumententyp, nicht Vertriebsweg** — PAS wird trotz Name gekauft — Muster: Marketing-Umklammern („PAS = free“ — falsch — Webstore: „Buy“) — [standards/IEC-PAS-62443-2-2-2025](standards/IEC-PAS-62443-2-2-2025.md).
2. **Langsamkeit/Verschiebungen:** Consensus, Phasen (PWI → … → PPUB), SMB, Target Dates 36–72 Monate — Ausgleichshebel sind formal.
3. **„Slow Standards = Critical Mass“:** „Standards with slow development include significant references and careful interpretation“ — „Where a requirement conflicts, newer takes precedence“ — längere Time-to-Market.
4. **Private-Charakter (Member-States):** ANSI (North America) vs IEC (global) — „The United States is one of the few developed nations without a presence in ISO management“ — [OECD — Case of ISO].
5. **„Unethical behavior and conflict of interests“** — ISO-History (Outcome.co).

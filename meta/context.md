# Standards Context — Types, Bodies and Terminology

This note consolidates the background needed to interpret the catalog. It is a
short, attributed summary of public sources; it is not the project's legal or
policy position.

## Publication types

| Type | Abbr. | Meaning | Process note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| International Standard | IS | Normative, consensus-approved | `processes/international-standards.md` |
| Technical Specification | TS | Detailed, but not through all approval stages | `processes/specifications.md` |
| Publicly Available Specification | PAS | Fast document for evolving technology; "publicly available" is a type, not free access | `processes/publicly-available-specifications.md` |

## Standards bodies — short comparison

| Body | Character | Scope | Access |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IEC | International, non-governmental | Electrical, electronic and related technologies | Members; Webstore |
| ISO | International, non-governmental | All sectors except electrical/electronic (with IEC overlap) | Members; catalog |
| ANSI | US private, non-profit | US representative to ISO/IEC; accreditation | Public lists; Webstore |
| ITU | UN specialized agency | Telecommunications | Recommendations (many free) |

Sources: IEC/ISO/ANSI public pages and the references below. The IEC and ISO
together account for roughly 85% of international product standards (Oxford,
*Demystifying private sector standards bodies*).

## External commentary

Public criticism of standards bodies appears in the referenced sources. The
recurring patterns are:

- development is described as slow and bureaucratic (Cambridge; Wikipedia);
- the private, member-state character of the large bodies raises questions
  about strategic competition (USS; Outcome.co);
- the "publicly available" label in PAS is a document type, not free access.

These are short summaries with links; no extended quotations are reproduced.
IEC's own process changes (e.g., shorter target dates) are part of the same
debate. See `processes/work-programme.md` for the target-date table.

## Key terms

- **Consensus** — two-thirds of P-members in favour, less than 25% negative,
  major opposition addressed.
- **CDV / FDIS** — enquiry draft / final draft International Standard.
- **PPUB / APUB** — publication issued / approved for publication.
- **RLV** — Redline Version (official text plus marked changes).
- **COR1 / AMD2** — corrigendum / amendment.
- **SDG anchor** — Sustainable Development Goal tags from the product page.

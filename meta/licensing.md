# Lizenzierung & Erlaubnisse (für den Ansatz an IEC)

Alle zitierten Inhalte stammen aus offiziellen IEC-Seiten, IEC-Open-PDFs oder verifizierten Aggregatoren (s. `meta/sources.md`).

## IEC Webstore AGBs (Stand 2025-08-04) — https://webstore.iec.ch/en/terms-conditions/

Wichtige Klauseln für diesen Vault:

1. **Urheberrecht** („Copyright and other intellectual property rights“):
   > „All IEC Products sold through IEC Webstore are protected by copyright pursuant to Swiss and international copyright laws. You shall not copy, transfer, sell, license, lease, give, modify, publish, assign, transmit or otherwise reproduce, disclose, make available to others or create derivative works from IEC Products, or any portion thereof, without permission in writing from IEC.“

   → Im Vault: **keine Volltexte, keine Nachbildungen der Docs**, nur Metadaten, kurze Zitate, Link-Container, Definitionen aus offiziellen IEC-Seiten.

2. **Kein Scraping/robots**:
   > „This prohibition includes the use of robots, scripts, or any other manual or automatic mechanism to data mine or scrape IEC Products without express written permission by IEC.“

   → Browser-Recherche (manuell) ist erlaubt; automatisierter Crawl des Webstores braucht schriftliche Erlaubnis.

3. **Keine Nutzung in KI/AI-Tools**:
   > „You shall not use publications and documents sold through IEC Webstore for the development of any algorithm, digital model, software program, or AI tool, including for the development or operation of a machine learning or AI system.“
   > „You shall not, and shall not permit others to, introduce and/or otherwise use IEC Products and any intellectual property associated with them in any manner in AI technologies unless you have obtained the specific and express permission of IEC.“

   → Der Prototyp ist „AI-Ready“-Barriere-freundlich: Metadaten/Abstracts/Link-Container sind **keine IEC-Dokumente**; für MCP/Exposure (z. B. vault-backed LLM-Tools) die Tool-Nutzung IECs per **Schriftlicher Erlaubnis** klären.

4. **Webstore-FAQ – Free newsletter**: „Stay up to date with the free IEC Just Published newsletter. This email will be sent on the 1st each month and will contain all publications issued the previous month.“ (echtes Geschenk von IEC).

## Affiliate-Programm — https://www.prd.iec.ch/acp/standardization-affiliates

> „It is prohibited to make, or allow others, to make available free of charge IEC International Standards and other publications, their respective adoptions and translations.“
> „The IEC also produces other publications such as technical specifications, technical reports, guides, industry technical agreements (ITA) and publicly available specifications.“

→ Wenn IEC-Standards „free“ werden, ggf. Affiliate-Regeln prüfen; frühzeitig mit IEC sprechen, um den „kostenlos verfügbar“-Bezug nicht zu verletzen.

## Elektropedia — https://www.electropedia.org/iev/iev.nsf/Welcome

> „While Electropedia’s structure and content are copyright IEC, we encourage the use, referencing or citation of Electropedia for the purpose of identifying or clarifying the meaning of electrotechnical concepts, terms and symbols… We only ask that IEC is referenced as the source. For users wishing to duplicate Electropedia or use large portions for commercially or for free sharing, please contact the IEC.“

→ Im Vault: kurze Zitate mit IEC-Namensnennung („Quintelag: IEC/Electropedia“), Verweis auf Live-Service (Link); Bulk-Import des 22k-Terms → mit IEC klären („duplicate Electropedia or use large portions … please contact the IEC“).

## Library Server — https://library.iec.ch/

> „Depending of your access rights you may only have access to specific headers / collections. Reminder for National Committees and appointed Sales Outlets: the publications can be used only for the purpose of national adoptions and sales.“

→ Member-Login zum Vollkatalog („all publications“, „Replaced / Withdrawn“).

## Status

- Der Vault enthält **keine IEC-Docs** (Urheberrecht), keine scraping-gestützten Daten, keine AI-Nutzung von IEC-Produkten (vgl. AGB).
- Zitate/Definitionen/Metadaten/Links aus offiziellen IEC-Seiten/Open-PDFs sind kurz, faktenbasiert, mit Quellnachweis (`meta/sources.md`, Frontmatter).
- Spätere Public-/AI-Entwicklung: **Anfrage an IEC** via https://webstore.iec.ch/en/contact/ mit Vorab-Diskussion (AI-Use-Permission Request, scraping-Permission Request).

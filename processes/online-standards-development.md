# Online Standards Development (OSD) — IEC Platform

Source: <https://iec.ch/online-standards-development>

## Purpose

International standards are developed through global cooperation and consensus, involving
technical experts, national mirror committee members and IEC staff. The OSD platform is
designed to let experts focus on content rather than formatting and to produce structured,
semantically rich standards.

## Capabilities

- A single environment for drafting, reviewing, consensus building and commenting.
- Integrated comment resolution and workflow support.
- Integration into the wider IEC ecosystem.

# ISO/IEC Directives — Public Reference Documents

The ISO/IEC Directives describe the technical procedures for developing and
maintaining standards. The IEC publishes the relevant documents as public PDFs.

| Document | Link |
| --- | --- |
| ISO/IEC Directives Part 1 + IEC Supplement (2024-05) | <https://www.iec.ch/system/files/2024-05/consolidated_iso-iec_part-1_iecsupplement_2024.pdf> |
| Stage codes IS/TS v2 | <https://iec.ch/system/files/2023-05/StageCodes_IS_v2.pdf> |
| Development and maintenance flow (IEC Academy webinar) | <https://www.iec.ch/system/files/2020-07/Convenor-BestPrac_Webinar_IEC-Academy_2020-04-08.pdf> |
| ISO Directives Part 2 (definitions) | <https://www.iso.org/sites/directives/current/part2/index.xhtml> |

## How this vault uses them

- The vault links to the public PDFs and records short factual extracts (stage
  names, stage codes, definitions) in
  [`development-stages.md`](development-stages.md) and
  [`international-standards.md`](international-standards.md).
- The vault does **not** reproduce the Directives or embed the PDFs.
- For any reuse beyond short attribution, check the terms of the respective
  organization and, where required, request permission.

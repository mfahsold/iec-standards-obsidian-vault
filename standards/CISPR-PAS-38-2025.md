## Description

CISPR Publicly Available Specification 38, CISPR PAS 38:2025.

“Industrial, scientific and medical equipment – Radio-frequency disturbance characteristics – Requirements for radio beam wireless power transfer (RB-WPT) equipment.”

Applies to radio beam wireless power transfer (RB-WPT) as industrial, scientific, and medical equipment operating in the frequency range of 0 Hz to 400 GHz and designed to operate up to a distance of 10 m. Covers emission requirements related to radio-frequency disturbances in the frequency range of 150 kHz to 18 GHz.

## Notes

- RB-WPT = Radio-Beam Wireless Power Transfer — areas “Electrical”/“Telecommunications”.
- “This PAS adopts the requirements from CISPR 11 and provides important clarifications on the application of CISPR 11 for RB-WPT.”

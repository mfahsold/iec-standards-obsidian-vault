# IEC 61675-2:2026

**Stand:** 2026-11-19 — data from official IEC Webstore pages (Just Published). No document bodies (copyright IEC — see `meta/licensing.md`).

## Description

IEC 61675-2:2026 describes terminology and test methods for gamma cameras with parallel-hole collimators (planar imaging) and adds tests for wholebody SPECT performance, quantitative SPECT image accuracy and pixelated semiconductor detector systems (hybrid CT). It cancels and replaces the 2015 edition as a technical revision, aligning with NEMA NU 1-2018 and removing obsolete SPECT-in-coincidence-mode tests (market share). Scope: gantry, single or multiple detector heads, scintillation crystals or solid-state detectors; new multi-head designs without planar acquisition are out of scope.

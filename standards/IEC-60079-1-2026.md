# IEC 60079-1:2026

**Stand:** 2026-11-19 — data from official IEC Webstore pages (Just Published). No document bodies (copyright IEC — see `meta/licensing.md`).

## Description

IEC 60079-1:2026 sets construction and testing requirements for products in explosive gas atmospheres, Type of Protection: flameproof enclosure "d". It supplements and modifies the general requirements of IEC 60079‑0 (the document takes precedence on conflicts) and is the eighth edition — a technical revision. Interpretation sheets can be published for clarifications and are available from the IEC webstore (history tab).

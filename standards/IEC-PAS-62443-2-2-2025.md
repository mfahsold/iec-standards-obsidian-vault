## Description

IEC Publicly Available Specification 62443-2-2, IEC PAS 62443-2-2:2025.

“Security for industrial automation and control systems – Part 2-2: IACS security protection scheme.”

Provides guidance on developing, validating, operating and maintaining a Security Protection Scheme (SPS) for industrial automation and control systems — mechanisms and procedures for asset owners to manage cyberthreat-related risks of the IACS in their facilities, based on other documents of the IEC 62443 series.

## Notes

- PAS documents “speed up standardization in areas of rapidly evolving technology” (see `processes/publicly-available-specifications.md`).
- IACS = Industrial Automation and Control Systems.

## Description

IEC Publicly Available Specification 62443-2-2, IEC PAS 62443-2-2:2025.

“Security for industrial automation and control systems – Part 2-2: IACS security protection scheme.”

Provides guidance on the development, validation, operation, and maintenance of a set of technical, physical, and process security measures called Security Protection Scheme (SPS). The document’s goal is to provide the asset owner implementing an IACS Security Program (SP) with mechanisms and procedures to ensure that the design, implementation and operation of an SPS manage the risks resulting from cyberthreats to each of the IACS included in its operating facility. The document is based on contents specified in other documents of the IEC 62443 series and explains how those contents can be used to support the development of technical, physical, and process security measures addressing the risks to the IACS during the operation phase.

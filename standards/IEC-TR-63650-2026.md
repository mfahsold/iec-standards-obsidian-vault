# IEC TR 63650:2026

**Stand:** 2026-11-19 — product page (Just Published). No document bodies (copyright IEC — see `meta/licensing.md`).

## Description

IEC Technical Report 63650, IEC TR 63650:2026.

“Electrochemical capacitor for use in electrical energy storage.”

Surveys the main technological approaches to electrochemical capacitors (supercapacitors) — current applications, characteristics in Electrical Energy Storage (EES) and representative application cases. Analyses the gap between existing IEC/ISO standards and the needs of EES applications and proposes a roadmap for future standardization. Also lists test items for cells and modules.

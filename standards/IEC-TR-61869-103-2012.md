**Data as of:** 2026-11-19 — product page (Webstore). No document bodies (copyright IEC — see `docs/provenance.md`).

## Description

IEC Technical Report 61869-103, IEC/TR 61869-103:2012(E).

“Instrument transformers - The use of instrument transformers for power quality measurement.”

Gives guidance on using instrument transformers (inductive and electronic) with electrical measuring instruments to measure and interpret power quality parameters in 50/60 Hz supply systems: power frequency, magnitude of supply voltage and current, flicker, supply voltage dips and swells, voltage interruptions, transients, supply voltage unbalance, voltage and current harmonics/interharmonics, mains signalling on the supply voltage and rapid voltage changes — including usage of HV instrument transformers.

## Beschreibung

IEC TR 62380, „Reliability data handbook – Universal model for reliability prediction of electronics components, PCBs and equipment“.

„The main task of IEC technical committees is to prepare International Standards. However, a technical committee may propose the publication of a technical report when it has collected data of a different kind from that which is normally published as an International Standard, for example 'state of the art'. IEC 62380, which is a technical report, has been prepared by IEC technical committee 47: Semiconductor devices.“

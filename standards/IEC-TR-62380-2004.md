## Description

IEC TR 62380, “Reliability data handbook – Universal model for reliability prediction of electronics components, PCBs and equipment”.

Technical reports are used by IEC technical committees to publish data of a different kind than International Standards (e.g., 'state of the art'), in contrast to the main task of IEC TCs preparing International Standards — IEC TR 62380 was prepared by IEC technical committee 47: Semiconductor devices.

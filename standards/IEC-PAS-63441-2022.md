## Description

IEC Publicly Available Specification 63441, IEC PAS 63441:2022.

“Functional architecture of industrial internet system for industrial automation applications.”

Defines the functional architecture and functional model of the Industrial Internet System for industrial applications — models, structures, activities and interaction contents between the end/edge/cloud layers: IaaS, PaaS and SaaS services respectively.

# Electropedia — IEV Online (IEV, IEC 60050)

Sources:

- <https://www.electropedia.org/iev/iev.nsf/Welcome>
- <https://www.electropedia.org/>
- <https://iec.ch/electropedia>

## What it is

Electropedia is the online version of the International Electrotechnical
Vocabulary (IEV, IEC 60050). It is produced by the IEC and contains more than
22,000 terminological entries in multiple languages. Responsibility lies with
IEC Technical Committee 1 (Terminology).

The vocabulary is free to consult online. Coverage varies by subject area and
language.

## Languages

The IEV includes entries in Arabic, Chinese, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch,
English, Finnish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Mongolian,
Norwegian Bokmål, Norwegian Nynorsk, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Serbian,
Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, and Ukrainian, among others.

## Usage terms

Electropedia’s structure and content are copyright IEC. The IEC encourages
referencing and citing Electropedia to identify or clarify electrotechnical
concepts, and asks that IEC be referenced as the source. Duplicating
Electropedia or using large portions of it requires contacting the IEC.

## How this vault uses it

- Links to Electropedia and the IEC page.
- Includes short, attributed references where a term is relevant.
- Does **not** bulk-import the 22,000+ entries.
- Does **not** reproduce definitions at length.

If you plan to build a larger term index, contact the IEC first. See
[`../docs/iec-engagement.md`](../docs/iec-engagement.md).

## Why it matters for retrieval

Electropedia is useful for resolving terminology before searching the catalog:
a question may use a synonym or a translated term that does not appear in a
standard title. A future extension could map terms to standards, but only with
appropriate permission and attribution.

## 1. Publikations-Typen (Definitionen)

| Typ | Abk. | Definiert von | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| International Standard | IS | https://iec.ch/publications/international-standards | „A normative document, developed according to consensus procedures, which has been approved by the IEC National Committee members of the responsible committee in accordance with Part 1 of the ISO/IEC Directives.“ |
| Technical Specification | TS | https://iec.ch/publications/specifications | „A technical specification approaches an international standard in terms of detail and completeness but has not yet passed through all approval stages… The final vote takes place at the draft technical specification (DTS) stage… approved by 2/3 of participating IEC Members (P-members).“ |
| Technical Report | TR | https://iec.ch/publications/technical-reports | „Entirely informative, not normative… approved by simple majority of participating IEC Member countries.“ |
| Publicly Available Specification | PAS | https://iec.ch/publications/specifications | „Speed up standardization in areas of rapidly evolving technology… approved by simple majority of the participating IEC Members of a TC/SC.“ |
| ISH — Interpretation Sheet | ISH | https://iec.ch/standards-development/workflows | Public-Workflows-Übersicht (Page listet IS/TS/TR/ISH/PAS/IEV); Definition nicht hier (keine verifizierte Seite in dieser Crawl-Welle). |
| IEV — IEC 60050 Vocabulary | IEV | https://www.electropedia.org/ | Kostenloser Online-Vokabular (IEV Online). |
